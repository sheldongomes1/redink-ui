# RedInk — Video Storyboards

## Version 1: The Equity Analyst Cut
**Audience:** Junior to mid-level equity analyst. Sent to course instructors.
**Length:** 90 seconds
**Tone:** Tommy Shelby — mystery, build, reveal
**Goal:** Make the analyst feel RedInk would have caught something they missed

---

### Scene 1 — The Hook (0–10s)
**Visual:** Black screen. White text fades in slowly, one line at a time.
**Narration / On-screen text:**
> "Every quarter, hundreds of 10-Q filings land on the desk of a junior analyst."
> *(pause)*
> "Most of them look identical."

**Cut to:** RedInk app loading — the anomaly list panel appearing from darkness.

---

### Scene 2 — The Problem (10–22s)
**Visual:** The left panel list. Rows appearing one by one, scores highlighted in red.
**Narration:**
> "The question has never been whether to read them all.
> It's which ones are worth your time."
> *(pause)*
> "Thirty companies. Sixty-five extreme anomalies across 1,297 filings. One model. One score."

**Visual:** Score badges pulse gently — 98.2, 97.8, 97.8...

---

### Scene 3 — The Selection (22–38s)
**Visual:** Cursor moves deliberately down the list, past SHOP, GOOG, GOOGL — and lands on WBD.
**Click.** The detail panel slides in.

**Narration:**
> "Warner Bros. Discovery. Quarter ending June 2022."
> *(pause)*
> "Score: one hundred."
> "The highest signal this model is capable of producing."

**Visual:** The score badge — bold red **100** — holds on screen for two full seconds.

---

### Scene 4 — The Evidence (38–58s)
**Visual:** Driver bars animate in. Revenue Growth ↑ MAX. Asset Growth ↑ MAX. Net Margin ↓ Extreme.
**Narration:**
> "Revenue had grown two hundred and twenty-one percent year over year."
> "Assets had expanded at the same extraordinary rate."
> "Both numbers were as far from normal as the model is capable of registering."
> *(beat)*
> "And beneath all of it — net margin had collapsed to negative thirty-four point eight percent."

**Visual:** The -34.8% metric card holds. Red. Steady.

---

### Scene 5 — The Analysis (58–74s)
**Visual:** Scroll down to the Analysis section. "The Flag" paragraph appears.
**Narration:**
> "The filing had been public for eleven days."
> "The merger with Discovery was well known."
> "What was not well known — was what the merger was hiding."
> *(pause)*
> "RedInk tells you not just what was detected."
> "It tells you exactly where to look."

**Visual:** "What to Look For" bullet points appear one by one.

---

### Scene 6 — The Filing Link (74–84s)
**Visual:** The "View SEC Filing →" button. Cursor hovers. Then clicks.
**Narration:**
> "Every flag links directly to the source."
> "No black boxes. No trading signals. Just the evidence — and a direct line to the filing."

---

### Scene 7 — The Close (84–90s)
**Visual:** Pull back to the full two-panel view. Clean. Steady.
**Narration:**
> "The ones worth your time — are already marked."

**Visual:** RedInk logo + "redink-ui-prod.web.app" fades in.
**Fade to black.**

## Version 2: The Hiring Manager Cut
**Audience:** PM, hiring manager, or senior reviewer evaluating this as a portfolio piece.
**Length:** 60 seconds
**Tone:** Confident, precise, product-forward — still Tommy Shelby, but showing the craft
**Goal:** Demonstrate product thinking, technical depth, and execution quality in 60 seconds

---

### Scene 1 — The Setup (0–8s)
**Visual:** RedInk loads. Full two-panel view.
**On-screen text (no narration):**
> "I built a financial anomaly detection tool for equity analysts."
> "No Bloomberg. No AlphaSense. Just the signal — and the evidence behind it."

---

### Scene 2 — The Architecture (8–20s)
**Visual:** Pan slowly from left panel (ranked anomaly list) to right panel (detail view).
**On-screen text:**
> "1,297 QQQ filings. Mahalanobis distance scoring. Pre-generated AI analyst narratives."
> "Every flag is explained. Every explanation is linked to the source."

**Visual:** Trust chips shown clearly — "8 features used · Model: brick3_q_v2 · Filing link ✓"

---

### Scene 3 — The Live Demo (20–40s)
**Visual:** Click WBD. Score 100 badge appears. Driver bars animate.
**On-screen text:**
> "Score: 100. Revenue +221% YoY. Net margin: -34.8%."
> *(scroll to Analysis)*
> "The model caught the WBD merger quarter — revenue surge, balance sheet expansion, profitability collapse."
> "The AI narrative was pre-generated. Eval-first. No hallucination risk at runtime."

---

### Scene 4 — Dataset Insights (40–50s)
**Visual:** Click "Dataset Insights" tab. Insights panel loads — stat cards, charts, serial flaggers.
**On-screen text:**
> "Pattern analysis across the full dataset."
> "Serial flaggers. Driver combinations. Peak quarters. Built from a Python analysis of 1,297 rows."

---

### Scene 5 — The Stack (50–58s)
**Visual:** Pull back to show the clean, full app.
**On-screen text:**
> "React · Tailwind · PapaParse · Firebase Hosting"
> "GitHub Actions CI/CD · Vitest · ESLint · Custom AI skills"
> "Prototype → Code review → Merge → Auto-deploy"

---

### Scene 6 — The Close (58–60s)
**Visual:** RedInk logo. Steady.
**On-screen text:**
> "Built in one weekend."

**Fade to black.**

## Production Notes

### For both versions:
- Record at **1280×720** minimum
- Use the **live app** at redink-ui-prod.web.app for authenticity
- Cursor movements should be **slow and deliberate** — never rushed
- Pause **2 full seconds** on key numbers (score 100, -34.8%)
- Driver bar animations should be **visible** — navigate to WBD fresh each time

### Equity Analyst version:
- Add light ambient music underneath — instrumental, understated (think Hans Zimmer Interstellar, not upbeat SaaS demo music)
- Voiceover tone: measured, unhurried, slight gravitas

### Hiring Manager version:
- No music needed — the precision of the product speaks
- On-screen text only (no voiceover) works well for async LinkedIn/portfolio viewing

### Recording order:
1. Record equity analyst version first (WBD walkthrough)
2. Then hiring manager version (full app + insights tab)
3. Export separately
